Amrinderjit Singh, LCSW is licensed in New York and provides forensic immigration mental health evaluations — VAWA, asylum, U-Visa, T-Visa, I-601/I-601A extreme hardship, and cancellation of removal — by secure telehealth for clients located in New York. He interviews clients directly in English, Punjabi, or Hindi and delivers reports to attorneys in 10–15 business days.

Immigration evaluations for New York attorneys

Amrinderjit Singh, LCSW is based in Richmond Hill, Queens — home to one of the largest Punjabi and Sikh communities in the United States — and has conducted immigration mental health evaluations for New York attorneys since opening his private practice.

New York attorneys refer clients from all five boroughs, Long Island, Westchester, the Hudson Valley, and upstate. Much of that work comes from Queens neighborhoods such as Richmond Hill, South Ozone Park, and Jamaica, and from Long Island communities with large South Asian populations. Because evaluations are held over secure video, clients anywhere in New York State can be seen without travel.

Evaluations are prepared both for USCIS filings — VAWA self-petitions, U-Visa and T-Visa petitions, and I-601A waivers — and for removal proceedings in New York's immigration courts, where asylum and cancellation of removal cases are common.

Licensure and telehealth in New York

Amrinderjit Singh holds an active LCSW license issued through the New York State Education Department, Office of the Professions. Evaluations are conducted over HIPAA-compliant video, so the client must be physically located in New York — or in another state where Singh is licensed — during the session. There is no need to travel to an office.

How a referral works

  1. Contact — call or text (516) 366-0247 or email Support@InderTherapy.com with the client's name, evaluation type, language, and deadline. Same-business-day response.
  2. Schedule — the client completes a brief intake and books a 60–90 minute telehealth session. A 50% deposit reserves the appointment.
  3. Evaluate — a one-on-one clinical interview in English, Punjabi, or Hindi, with validated screening instruments.
  4. Deliver — the report goes securely to the attorney within 10–15 business days, or 7 business days expedited.

Frequently asked questions

Do you conduct immigration evaluations for clients in New York?

Yes. Amrinderjit Singh holds an active New York LCSW license and evaluates clients physically located in New York by secure video.
