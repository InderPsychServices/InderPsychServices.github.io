Immigration evaluations for New Jersey attorneys

Amrinderjit Singh, LCSW holds an active New Jersey LCSW license and provides immigration mental health evaluations for New Jersey attorneys and their clients by secure telehealth.

New Jersey has some of the most concentrated South Asian communities in the country — in Middlesex County towns such as Edison and Iselin, in Carteret, and across Hudson and Somerset counties. Many clients in these communities are more comfortable describing trauma, abuse, or family hardship in Punjabi or Hindi, and Singh conducts those interviews himself with no interpreter.

New Jersey removal cases are heard at the Newark and Elizabeth immigration courts. Attorneys regularly request hardship evaluations for cancellation of removal and I-601A waivers, and trauma evaluations for asylum, VAWA, and U-Visa matters.

Licensure and telehealth in New Jersey

Amrinderjit Singh holds an active LCSW license issued through the New Jersey State Board of Social Work Examiners. Evaluations are conducted over HIPAA-compliant video, so the client must be physically located in New Jersey — or in another state where Singh is licensed — during the session. There is no need to travel to an office.

How a referral works

  1. Contact — call or text (516) 366-0247 or email Support@InderTherapy.com with the client's name, evaluation type, language, and deadline. Same-business-day response.
  2. Schedule — the client completes a brief intake and books a 60–90 minute telehealth session. A 50% deposit reserves the appointment.
  3. Evaluate — a one-on-one clinical interview in English, Punjabi, or Hindi, with validated screening instruments.
  4. Deliver — the report goes securely to the attorney within 10–15 business days, or 7 business days expedited.

Frequently asked questions

Do you conduct immigration evaluations for clients in New Jersey?

Yes. Amrinderjit Singh holds an active New Jersey LCSW license and evaluates clients physically located in New Jersey by secure video.

Do New Jersey clients need to travel to New York for the evaluation?

No. Evaluations are held by secure video, so New Jersey clients join from home or from their attorney's office while physically located in New Jersey.

What evaluations are available in New Jersey?

VAWA, asylum (including withholding and CAT), U-Visa, T-Visa, I-601 and I-601A extreme hardship waivers, and cancellation of removal evaluations.

How fast can I get a report?

Standard turnaround is 10–15 business days from the evaluation. Seven-business-day expedited service is available for an additional fee.
