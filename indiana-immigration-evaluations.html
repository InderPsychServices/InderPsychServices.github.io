Immigration evaluations for Indiana attorneys

Amrinderjit Singh, LCSW holds an active Indiana LCSW license and conducts immigration mental health evaluations by telehealth for Indiana attorneys and their clients.

Indianapolis and its suburbs, including Greenwood and Fishers, have a large and fast-growing Punjabi and Sikh community, along with Hindi-speaking families across the state. Many recent arrivals are more comfortable discussing trauma and family hardship in Punjabi, and Singh conducts those interviews directly.

Indiana removal cases are heard at the Indianapolis Immigration Court. Asylum evaluations are a frequent referral, along with cancellation of removal, VAWA, U-Visa, and I-601A hardship evaluations.

Licensure and telehealth in Indiana

Amrinderjit Singh holds an active LCSW license issued through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, Behavioral Health and Human Services Licensing Board. Evaluations are conducted over HIPAA-compliant video, so the client must be physically located in Indiana — or in another state where Singh is licensed — during the session. There is no need to travel to an office.

How a referral works

  1. Contact — call or text (516) 366-0247 or email Support@InderTherapy.com with the client's name, evaluation type, language, and deadline. Same-business-day response.
  2. Schedule — the client completes a brief intake and books a 60–90 minute telehealth session. A 50% deposit reserves the appointment.
  3. Evaluate — a one-on-one clinical interview in English, Punjabi, or Hindi, with validated screening instruments.
  4. Deliver — the report goes securely to the attorney within 10–15 business days, or 7 business days expedited.

Frequently asked questions

Do you conduct immigration evaluations for clients in Indiana?

Yes. Amrinderjit Singh holds an active Indiana LCSW license and evaluates clients physically located in Indiana by secure video.

Do you conduct Punjabi-language evaluations for Indiana clients?

Yes. Singh is a native Punjabi speaker and conducts Indiana evaluations directly in Punjabi, Hindi, or English by secure video.
