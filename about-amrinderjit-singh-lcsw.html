Amrinderjit Singh, LCSW is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker based in Richmond Hill, Queens, New York, who conducts forensic mental health evaluations for immigration cases. He is licensed in NY, NJ, CA, WA, MI, WI, IN, PA, and VA and interviews clients directly in English, Punjabi, and Hindi.

Background

Amrinderjit (Inder) Singh is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker whose practice focuses on forensic mental health evaluations for immigration proceedings. He has personally conducted and written more than 500 evaluations for attorneys representing clients in VAWA, asylum, U-Visa, T-Visa, hardship waiver, and cancellation of removal matters.

A native Punjabi speaker who is also fluent in Hindi, he grew his practice in Richmond Hill, Queens, and serves South Asian immigrant communities across the country by telehealth. He conducts every evaluation and writes every report himself.

Active LCSW licensure in New York, New Jersey, California, Washington, Michigan, Wisconsin, Indiana, Pennsylvania, and Virginia.

Approach

Evaluations are trauma-informed, grounded in validated screening instruments and DSM-5-TR criteria, and written in plain language for immigration adjudicators. Singh keeps a clear line between clinical findings and legal conclusions, which is what makes a report credible to USCIS officers and immigration judges.

Languages

English, Punjabi, and Hindi — spoken directly. Urdu and Spanish through a qualified interpreter. See Punjabi and Hindi evaluations.
